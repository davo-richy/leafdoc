    How LeafDoc works

    1. Take one to four clear photos: the problem close up, the whole plant, and the underside of a leaf.
    2. Add a few words about what you see, or say them out loud.
    3. Read the likely problems, treatment and prevention. Answer follow-up questions to sharpen the result.

    LeafDoc gives guidance, not a lab test. Photos go to Google Gemini for analysis and are not stored on our server.
